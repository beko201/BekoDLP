# Third-party licenses

BekoDLP's own code is licensed under [GPL-3.0-only](../LICENSE). Third-party
libraries and tools retain their respective licenses and copyright notices.

See [Libraries and sources](libraries-and-sources.md) for upstream project links.
Binary distributions include third-party notices in the `licenses/` folder:

- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`: component overview and acknowledgements.
- `NOTICES.txt`: collected license and notice texts.
- `NOTICE_INDEX.json`: mappings between components and their notices.

Credits and upstream links do not replace license texts or applicable
corresponding-source requirements. The available third-party source and notice
records are incomplete; this document does not certify distribution compliance.

When distributing a binary, provide its matching application source and the
third-party notices and source materials required by the included components.
